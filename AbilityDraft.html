Arcade Protocol // 01

Ability Draft

  1. Recruit.Buy heroes from the Hero Shop. They arrive with no ability.
  2. Select.Click a hero on your six-slot board to open their loadout channel.
  3. Draft.Install any offered ability, even if it belongs to another universe.
  4. Inspect.Hover, focus, or tap a unit to see its full stats, equipped powers, and every combat effect.
  5. Reposition.Drag heroes between board slots or use the Move / Swap control on mobile.
  6. Merge.Combine two matching heroes of the same level to increase power and health. Different equipped abilities fuse and remain active, up to level 4.
  7. Fight.Ready up or wait for the timer. Your custom builds battle an AI draft rival.
