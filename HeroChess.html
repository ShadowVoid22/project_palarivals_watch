Crownfall Draft // Field Manual

Chess, Rewritten

  1. Contest.Draft one unique hero for pawns, rooks, knights, bishops, queen, and king. The AI claims from the same pool.
  2. Manifest.Your six chosen identities are projected across a standard chess formation.
  3. Anomalies.Every hero has a different power for every possible chess role. Hover pieces to inspect the active effect.
  4. Shields.A shield blocks one capture attempt. The attack consumes the shield and the attacker remains in place.
  5. Check.You may not make a move that leaves your king threatened. Checkmate wins; stalemate draws.
  6. Protocol Limits.Pawn promotion is automatic. Castling and en passant are disabled inside the anomaly grid.
